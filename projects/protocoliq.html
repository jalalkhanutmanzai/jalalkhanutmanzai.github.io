Laboratory Software · Institute Edition

A connected workspace for the everyday work of a research laboratory.

ProtocolIQ brings laboratory calculations, chemical inventory, resource requests, equipment lending and protocol documentation into one web application. I developed it around the practical needs of research labs and educational institutions, with a setup that each institute can own and manage.

From a lab calculation to a shared workflow

A laboratory needs more than a calculator. Reagents belong to specific batches and labs, requests need approval, equipment needs a recorded borrower, and procedures need clear documentation. ProtocolIQ connects those steps while keeping access tied to individual roles and laboratory memberships.

Scientific calculations

Molarity, stock dilutions, unit conversions, percent solutions and preparation guidance, including final-volume top-up and an optional 10% extra batch.

Inventory & approvals

Batch quantities, storage locations, expiry and low-stock alerts. Request approval checks compatible units and records stock deduction and usage together.

Equipment & faults

Asset registration, check-out and return logs, fault reports and recorded resolutions. Unresolved faults prevent further lending in the application.

People & lab access

Institution, department and lab structure, with individual student, staff and administrator accounts. Guest access supports exploring the bench and assay guides.

Protocols & records

A personal SOP editor, assay reference library, PDF export, inventory CSVs, activity records and backups covering the institute's labs.

Optional lab assistant

Local calculation and stock routing, with an optional server-side Gemini or GROQ connection using authorized lab inventory. Each institute supplies its own provider configuration.

Designed for the receiving institute

A fresh installation opens a first-run wizard. The institute enters its name, departments, laboratories and its own administrator credentials, then adds team accounts and lab memberships. The default edition stores data on the institute's computer or server; a personal Google or Firebase account is not required.

Engineering & validation

The frontend keeps authentication, data management, scientific computation, chat routing and application control in separate JavaScript modules. A Node.js server and SQLite database handle the independent institutional edition. Optional Firebase support remains available for institutions choosing their own cloud setup.
