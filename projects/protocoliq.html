Built around the way a lab works

A preparation calculation is only one part of an experiment. Materials must be available, requests approved, equipment accounted for and procedures reviewed. ProtocolIQ connects these steps while enforcing the person's role and assigned laboratories on the server.

Calculate & prepare

Molarity, stock dilutions, unit conversions and solution preparation, with final-volume top-up guidance and an optional 10% extra batch.

Track & approve

Batch inventory, expiry and low-stock alerts, reasoned adjustments and a stock ledger. Approvals check units and deduct stock atomically, with retry and stale-edit protection.

Borrow & maintain

Equipment check-out and returns, borrower records, fault reporting, maintenance and calibration dates. Outstanding faults or overdue calibration block lending.

Write & review

Private SOP drafts, revision history and independent staff review before publication. Published procedures remain available while a new draft is prepared.

Protect access

Individual accounts, laboratory memberships and distinct role controls. Configurable OpenID Connect SSO, authenticator MFA, one-use recovery codes and session management.

Ask with lab context

A local calculation router and optional server-side Gemini or Groq assistant using authorized inventory context. The institute supplies its own provider credentials.

A workspace for each responsibility

Administrators manage the institute, staff run laboratory operations, and students focus on their own work. Interface controls reflect permissions enforced by the backend.

01 / ADMINISTRATOR

02 / LAB STAFF

Daily operations

03 / STUDENT & USER

The institute owns the setup

The first-run wizard creates an institute's departments, labs and first administrator. The administrator adds individual accounts and laboratory memberships. Local operations need no personal Google or Firebase account; optional AI and identity-provider connections use the institute's credentials.

Engineering behind the experience

The six-part frontend separates authentication, data, calculations, chat routing and application control. Node.js handles server authorization and provider calls. SQLite supports a single institute server; a PostgreSQL adapter supplies shared records and sessions for cloud deployments. Docker and HTTPS deployment templates accompany the delivery.

Verified delivery, with clear deployment boundaries

The 60-test suite covers scientific calculations, role and lab isolation, stock transactions, protocol review, session controls, encrypted secrets, audit integrity and MFA enrollment/recovery. PostgreSQL integration was exercised with a PostgreSQL-in-WASM database. The extracted delivery package starts with its bundled production dependencies, and admin/user panels were checked in the browser.

Live hosting, real identity-provider activation and multi-host load or failover testing are completed in the receiving institute's environment. Screens show demonstration accounts, not customer deployments. Laboratory procedures require the institution's own review and approved SOPs.
